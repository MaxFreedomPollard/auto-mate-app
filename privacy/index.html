Auto Mate privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026. Published by C60 LLC, the developer of Auto Mate. Contact: max@c60spaceship.com.

This policy is published at https://maxfreedompollard.github.io/auto-mate-app/privacy/ and inside the app under Settings.

The short version

Auto Mate listens through your microphone and streams that audio to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key. The app has no server of its own. Nothing you hear or say is sent to us. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking in the app.

What the app processes

Microphone audio. While a session is running, Auto Mate captures audio from your phone or headset microphone and streams it in real time to the AI provider you selected in Settings (for example xAI, OpenAI, Google, or a custom server you configured). The audio is not stored by Auto Mate. The provider processes it under the provider's own terms and privacy policy, which you accept when you create your account with them.

Speech recognition for text models. If you choose a text model instead of a realtime voice provider, Auto Mate does not send audio to that provider. Your device's speech recognition service turns what the microphone hears into text, and only that text goes to the text model. Auto Mate asks for recognition on the device; where the device cannot do that, the recognition service (for example Google's speech services) may process the audio on its own servers under its own privacy policy.

Text produced during a session. The provider returns text and, in some modes, speech. Auto Mate shows that text on screen and in the lock-screen notification while the session runs. Transcripts are kept in memory only, unless you turn on "Save transcripts on this device", in which case they are written to the app's private storage on your phone and can be exported or deleted from Settings.

Your brief and settings. The instructions you type (your brief), the verbosity level, translation settings and other preferences are stored on your device. Your brief is sent to the provider as part of the instructions for each session.

API keys. Keys you enter are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore and stored in the app's private storage. They are sent only to the provider they belong to, over HTTPS or secure WebSocket, unless you connect to a server on your own device or network with an unencrypted address as described below. They are never logged and never leave your device for any other destination.

Unencrypted local servers. Connections to providers are encrypted. If you turn on "Allow unencrypted local servers" (off by default), Auto Mate will also use plain http or ws addresses, but only for this device or a private network address such as a model server on your computer. Everything sent to such a server, including any key you set for it, travels over your network without encryption.

What the app does not do

  • No account with us, no sign-up, no server of ours.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No recording when a session is stopped. The persistent notification tells you when Auto Mate is listening.

Third parties

The only third parties that receive data are the AI providers you configure. Each is governed by its own policy:

  • xAI: https://x.ai/legal/privacy-policy
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google (Gemini API): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Any custom endpoint: whoever operates it
  • When you use a text model: your device's speech recognition service, under its own policy

If you use a model server on your own device or network, what Auto Mate sends to it stays on that network.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to capture audio for the session. Required.
  • Notifications: to show the listening notification with Pause and Stop controls. Required for background use.
  • Bluetooth connect: only if you choose a headset microphone, to route audio to it. Optional.
  • Foreground service (microphone and media playback types): to keep working with the screen locked.
  • Internet: to reach the provider you chose.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations vary by place and may require the consent of the people being recorded. You are responsible for using Auto Mate lawfully where you are.

Children

Auto Mate is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Delete saved transcripts in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Data already sent to a provider is held under that provider's policy and deletion process.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update and the effective date above is updated.
